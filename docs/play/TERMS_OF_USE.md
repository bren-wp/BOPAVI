## English

BOPAVI is an offline-playable arcade game for Android and iOS with a single active character, obstacle flight, score, berry collection, pause and results. Berries are not currency and cannot be redeemed for money or goods. There are no ads or real-money in-app purchases in the current product.

Records and preferences are stored locally, and historic save data may remain for compatibility. The current UI **does not** offer manual import/export or a Settings screen. Uninstalling does not necessarily erase files previously saved outside the app.
